Terms & Conditions Required
1. Entry & Eligibility

Each prediction entry costs a flat fee of $10 USD. Users must be 18 years or older to participate. EPOCH GDX reserves the right to verify identity before processing any payouts. Entries are non-refundable once confirmed.

2. Prize Distribution

The closest prediction to the verified event date wins the jackpot. Proximity tiers distribute a portion of the pool to near-winners within ±3, ±7, and ±14 days of the actual date. Prize pool = 70% of entry fees + $10,000 house seed. Distributed automatically once the event date is verified.

3. GDX Tokens

GDX Tokens are awarded to subscribers and can be redeemed for entry credits. Tokens have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Unused tokens expire 90 days after issuance.

4. Event Verification

All milestone dates are verified by EPOCH GDX's independent data panel using publicly verifiable sources. The panel's decision is final and binding. Disputes must be raised within 30 days of a pool closure.

5. Limitation of Liability

EPOCH GDX is not responsible for losses arising from technical failures, incorrect date entries, or external events beyond our control. Participation is at the user's own risk.

Overview

EPOCH GDX is the world's first date-based prediction platform. You pick the exact date a global milestone will occur — from Bitcoin hitting $100K to the next Moon landing. The closest prediction wins the jackpot. Proximity tiers reward near-winners. No house edge on outcomes — only the closest date wins.

01
◉
CHOOSE YOUR POOL

Browse 9 active prediction markets across Digital Assets, Finance, Technology, Space, and Global Statistics. Each pool has its own prize jackpot and entry window.

02
⊕
SPIN THE PREDICTION WHEEL

Move the Dial to the year you believe the event will occur. Optionally narrow it down to a specific day and month using the date picker below the wheel for a more precise — and rewarding — prediction.

03
◈
LOCK YOUR ENTRY

Each entry costs a flat $10. You can submit up to 3 entries per pool with different dates. Subscribers get up to 6 entries per pool. Entries are final once confirmed.

04
⬟
CLAIM YOUR JACKPOT

Once the milestone occurs, EPOCH's independent data panel verifies the exact date. Prizes are distributed automatically to the jackpot winner and proximity tier winners.

Prize Distribution Mechanism
SCENARIO A
Exact Date Winner

Exact date winner(s) split the full prize pool equally. Platform rake is 30%, deducted upfront. Winners split 100% of the prize pool (70% of gross + $10,000 house seed).

SCENARIO B
No Exact Winner

Pool splits in half. 50% distributed to Tier 1 (±3d), Tier 2 (±7d), Tier 3 (±14d) in priority order. 50% rolls over to active pools. Closer tiers take priority.

SCENARIO C
Jackpot Carry

No winner within ±14 days. Full prize pool converts to GDX Tokens returned to all participants. 1 entry = 1 token ($10 value), valid for 90 days.
